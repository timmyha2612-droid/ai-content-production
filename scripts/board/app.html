Director’s look book

Each reference has a label, like @Editor. Click it to rename. Prompts that mention @Editor point at that image: on Seedance it becomes @Image1, @Image2 and so on, in the order the images are sent. Add references with + Ref, by dropping images on an attribute, or by clicking its text and pasting. New references are Ref (reference only: they guide the prompt and are never sent). Tap the tag to mark one Owned, Licensed or Generated so it can become a Higgsfield input.
